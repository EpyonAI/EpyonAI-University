## Home
**Human–AI collaboration that opens real opportunities.**

Epyon AI University is an Atlanta-based educational nonprofit. We help people learn, build and pursue opportunity with AI guides, human coaching and practical community resources. Our first programs bring that work into career development, responsible AI education and accessible information.

Learning is only part of a pathway. A missing computer, transportation problem, unreliable internet connection or lack of time can stop someone before a course begins. Pathways Career Compass helps learners identify those barriers, find relevant resources and choose a route they can actually follow.

Primary actions: Explore Pathways · Volunteer · Support the mission.
Status: Building our first programs and inviting volunteers, donors and community partners.

## Pathways and Pathfinder
**A pathway built around the person.**

Epyon Pathways is our workforce-development program. Pathfinder is the application we are building to connect a learner's goals, demonstrated skills and practical needs with training, guidance and opportunities.

The journey begins with a goal. We examine what an opportunity requires, identify the learner's next skills and connect credible resources. We adapt instruction into manageable formats, support practice with an AI guide and human coaching, and collect evidence of what the learner can do.

Career Compass addresses access as well as learning: equipment, internet, transportation, food resources, schedules and local support. Referrals include what is known about eligibility, booking, cost and access so learners can prepare.

### Tax preparation and accounting
Prepare for entry-level tax operations and seasonal tax-preparation work through foundational instruction, source-document practice, secure workflows and supervised cases. Epyon Tax, Atlas, Legend and Microsoft Copilot workflow skills provide an initial domain environment. Client work requires appropriate supervision and current requirements; training does not confer a professional license.

### Personal training and fitness
Janus Fitness provides an approved ecosystem concept for a trainer pathway. We plan to work with an established certification program and select a partner that best serves Pathfinder learners. Pathfinder supports access, practice, persistence and navigation around that certification. Partner selection and placement arrangements are in development.

### Music and digital media
A music pilot inspired by Charlie's creative work combines AI-assisted composition with human performance, analog recording and editing tools such as Audacity. Learners practice production, rights awareness, release planning and the business of creative work. This will expand into a permanent route for musicians, artists and digital-media creators.

### Opportunity creation
EEON, the planned Epyon Employment Opportunity Network, will connect reviewed learner capabilities with jobs, contracts, clients and entrepreneurial routes. Where suitable opportunities are missing, we aim to help develop organizations and projects that can create them. Employment and earnings are not guaranteed.

## Libraries
**Make libraries cool again.**

Libraries can be a gateway to tools, creative work and community support. We are meeting library staff to understand what learners can use, how access works and what preparation is required.

We are seeking conversations with county and university libraries about computers, recording and podcasting facilities, creative software, animation, makerspaces, staff guidance and learning resources. Our first outreach targets include Fulton, Cobb and Gwinnett county systems and Georgia Tech, Emory and Georgia State.

These conversations are planned outreach. A library appearing in our research does not mean it is a partner or that every visitor can use every facility.

We verify eligibility, reservations, equipment, software, fees, training and accessibility before recommending a resource. Help us map what your library offers and design a useful learner referral.

Actions: Discuss a library partnership · View assessment questions · Volunteer as a resource scout.
